project supervised by Aaron Wetzler. No application source or executable is
available in this repository, and no current-device build is verified.

unchanged. Nofar supplied three additional original files on 2026-11-13:
a project overview in DOCX format, a one-slide poster PPTX and a 15-slide presentation PPTX
with an embedded WMV recording. The report in that collection exactly matches
the existing docs/report.pdf. Hashes and the document/media review scope are
in PROVENANCE.md. Document and media inspection does not reproduce application execution.

Nofar confirmed permission to keep/publish the project materials and supplied
https://nofar.carme.li/ plus a public YouTube link. Her response does not create
a repository-wide license or speak for other rights holders. Keep original
credits, third-party material and the missing-source qualification intact.
